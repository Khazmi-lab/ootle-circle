Ootle Circle

A rotating savings circle (jam'iya, tontine, susu, chama, tanda, committee) with no cash-holder. Everyone pays each round, one member takes the pot, collateral covers anyone who misses. This page is a walletless simulation of the on-chain template; every button shows the Ootle transaction it maps to.

The pot never sits with a person: only settle can move it, and only to the member whose turn it is. The organizer cannot withdraw funds. This demo does not connect to a wallet or a network.
